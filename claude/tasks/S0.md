## Findings that change the series' picture

1. **`npm run test:brep` was red on `main`, and nobody could tell.** Under Node ≥ 23 every test "failed" with a green tick. Fixed in `4971a29`.
2. **Pears from 0.05 to 0.40 ct could not be built at all.** Fixed in `f36345f`. Please review: it is a geometry change for designs that previously crashed.
3. **The STEP export silently drops a solid.** The six-prong marquise's metal is 2 solids, but its STEP holds one. This is why I9 is split (see the decisions below).
4. **OCCT's fuse alters its input shapes.** Anything measuring the unfused parts after `fuseMetal` can get the wrong answer. The audit measures a second, untouched build.
5. **Other things the baseline shows** (all reported, none binding yet):
   - side stones and centre intersect on three-stone rings at 3 ct (up to 0.17 mm³)
   - halo accents intersect (up to 0.58 mm³, marquise 3 ct)
   - half eternity drops a part in the fuse
   - pavé culets clear their seat floors by 0.01 mm
   - on a full eternity band, one accent sits under the head, with one OCCT intersection failing there (recorded as a measurement error)
