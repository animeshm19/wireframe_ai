# S1: stone seated in real bearings, as-cast prongs, culet clear

**Status: done**, with four decisions for Animesh (below). Branch `structure/ring-head`, start `088c607`. Run in Claude Code on the Mac, 2026-10-07.

## What changed

- **`src/lib/head-layout.ts`** (new, pure maths). From the cut, girdle radius, prong count and the head's effective bench values it lays out, in the stone's frame:
  - the stone's real girdle polygon;
  - a convex seat envelope: the upper hull of every stone vertex, grown by `seatTolerance` sideways and up and down;
  - each prong's girdle point and normal, and its post, set `bearingDepth`% of d inside the seat surface;
  - the post's as-cast height, `asCastProngHeight` above the table;
  - the leg, foot and interim rail.

  The legs are found by search against exact clearance bounds: the pavilion below girdle − d, the culet, the seat envelope, and any neighbouring stone.
- **Engine (`cad-engine-brep.ts`)**:
  - Bench values are resolved once per head, and the engine reports what it built (`parts.bench`, `heads[i].bench`, `heads[i].layout`).
  - As-cast prongs are a swept circle with a 0.02 mm flat cap instead of a pole, so the STL no longer has a zero-area triangle at each tip.
  - The seat is cut before the fuse with an envelope clipped to each post, checked like `cutAll`.
  - The culet is placed `culetClearance` above the band's own top on the head axis.
  - Feet land in the band as each head sees it: regions, tapers, profiles, and a split shank's slot.
  - The interim rail is a flat ring on the straight legs, and halos hang their bearers on it.
  - Three-stone side heads keep clear of the centre stone.
  - `fuseMetal` seals sealed cavities under 0.001 mm³.
  - The kernel warms its code at boot.
- **Audit**: per-prong bearing measurements by exact line probes. The invariant table gains I2c and I10a–c, and I13 measurements for the six S1 fields; I13 now ignores fields not yet wired at the stage asked.
- **Harness**: `?prongs=`.
