## What changed

### Architecture: (C), a saddle of its own under a base ring

The justification is in `BasePlan`'s comment in `head-layout.ts`.

- **The base.** A flat ring under the stone that follows its outline.
  - It is max(prongDiameter, minWall) tall.
  - It is wide enough for a foot leaning in at 45° to come up through its top with room for its fillet.
  - Every prong's foot ends in it, 0.1 mm under its top. No prong touches the band.
  - The culet points into its open middle.
- **The saddle.** A solid prism of the plate's outline, extruded from inside the band up to a flat plateau 0.03 mm under the band's crest.
  - The plate's outline is the base's outline, grown by at least 0.3 mm and drawn out along the band.
  - The base is sunk 0.1 mm into the plateau, so the two fuse with real overlap.
  - On a 1.2 mm band the plate is several times the band's width. On an 8 mm band it ends inside the band's width.
- **Why not (A), the shank's own section widened under the head.**
  - The shank is a single exact revolve on most rings. A section that changes under the head would loft every band, which is slower to build and to show.
  - A raised band top would also put metal under the culet.
- **Why not (B), a hollow cone up to the stone.** There is no height for one: the stone sits only culetClearance above the band.
- **Bore and light.**
  - Nothing of the saddle rises above the band's top. The bore is untouched (see criterion 4).
  - Between the base and the girdle only the prongs and the gallery stand. Light reaches the pavilion.

### Blends (`86f66d4`, `b8d68f5`)

- **The three joints.** `resolveRing` fillets, on the fused solid:
  - the feet to the base;
  - the base to the saddle;
  - the saddle's wall to the band.
- **The check on each fillet.** It must:
  - be valid and one solid;
  - add metal, but no more than a fillet can;
  - add faces.

  The feet are tried one prong at a time if all at once fails.
- **The fallback, designed in from the start.** Where a fillet fails or has no room, the joint gets a flare instead:
  - `flaredFoot`: a ruled cone up each foot;
  - `baseFlare` and `shankFlare`.

  Feet with no room for a fillet are built flared in the parts.
- **The mesh check.** The blended metal must mesh closed at the export's 0.01 mm. Until it does, each filleted joint is flared in turn. If it still does not close, the blend is marked `meshOpen` (see q. 37).
- **Radii laid out per joint.** filletRadius is held to 45% of the thinner part the fillet joins. On the feet it is also held to the room the base's top leaves round each foot. Every hold is reported as `engine-clamped` with the value asked and the value delivered.

### Size clamps (Q25, `252509a`, `3f810a2`)

Where the asked values cannot fit the stone, the engine holds them by its size and reports both numbers.
- **Culet gap.** Held to the most that lets the gallery hide under the girdle, in 0.05 mm steps.
- **Gallery gap.** Held towards the bench minimum while the gallery still shows.
- **Effect in the full matrix.** Only the 0.25 ct round all-max row is held:
  - culet 1.5 → **1.35** mm;
  - gallery gap 0.6 → **0.353** mm.
