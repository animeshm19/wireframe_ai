## What changed

**Gallery layout (`src/lib/head-layout.ts`)**
- **Centreline.** It is the girdle's section at the gallery height with every side set outward. The offset is solved per side so that a round wire of thickness t keeps galleryClearance (3D) from the stone:
  - on a brilliant, from the hull's planes;
  - on a step cut, from its real sections.
- **Corners.** At corners turning more than 30° (marquise and pear points, step-cut corners), bevels are added.
  - Each bevel is set off by the exact gap to the stone's faces.
  - The bevels are laid on an arc at least 0.1 mm wider than the wire.
  - The polygon is the half-plane intersection of all sides (`clipLines`), so a bevel that would not cut its corner drops out instead of folding the outline.
- **Folds.** No side may be shorter than the wire's mitres need (`tubeSideFits`). A corner too tight for the wire falls back to a plain mitre, which never folds.
- **Height.** The gallery sits at the rule-of-thirds height, pavH/2, where it hides under the girdle.
  - Otherwise it sits lower: searched in 0.03 mm steps, then refined to 0.002 mm, with 0.003 mm to spare from above.
  - It may go as low as the culet and band allow (`culetClearance` off the culet, 0.1 mm off the band).
  - A height is refused where any point of the wire comes nearer the stone than galleryClearance − 0.015.
- **Thinning.** Only where no height hides it, down to the alloy's minimum wall and never below. Both lowering and thinning are reported as `engine-clamped`, each under its real reason.
- **Legs.** Each leg runs post → bend → straight through the gallery centreline on the prong's normal → knee → foot. Changes to the search:
  - The crossing may sit further out along the normal, as far as 0.2 mm of overlap square to the wire allows. An emerald's corner prongs cross its chamfer side at 45°, where a step along the normal moves the leg only 0.71 of it off the wire.
  - **A new leg form, the "kicked" leg**, tried only where no straight leg keeps the pavilion gap. The leg steps out along the normal just under the seat, then runs back in through the wire (one bend more). First tried inside the I7 joint disc, then outside it. Needed by:
    - the deepest-bearing marquise (`bearingDepth` 50), whose post passes the pavilion at 0.13 mm (estimate) straight down from girdle − d;
    - the 0.25 ct princess's corner prongs, to land inside the joint.
  - A head without a gallery now lays its legs out as S1 did.
- **Speed.** The default ring's layout went from 32.8 to 21.0 ms and the oval's from 210.0 to 63.5 ms. Faces are prepared once and a plane-distance cut-off skips faces that cannot be nearest; bevels are solved once per height; the band under a foot is probed once per distance.

**Engine (`src/lib/cad-engine-brep.ts`)**
- **`galleryWire`** is the mitred tube from `585151e` (a ruled loft through mitre sections). It now:
  - refuses a centreline that would fold;
  - checks its volume against π(t/2)² × the centreline's length within 1%;
  - checks that it is one solid;
  - runs BRepCheck on it.
- **Seat assertion.** The seat envelope's cut of the wire must change it by at most 0.001 mm³, either way. Above that, the build throws.
- **`asCastProng`** refuses a spine whose bends overlap. That aborted the WASM kernel on a 0.25 ct three-stone.
- **Halo**, minimal attachment fixes; S6 redesigns it.
  - A seat the rail does not reach is tied to it by a short bar. An emerald halo came apart into 6 pieces once the interim rail was gone.
  - Bearers start on the gallery and end 0.2 accent radii past the rail's centreline. Before, they ended on its outer wall, which left 2 zero-area triangles on a marquise halo.
- **`fuseMetal`.** A fuse that leaves the metal in more pieces than before is retried with the part moved 0.001 mm along each axis.
  - The retry is taken only as an exact union that really joins: rv = vol + pv − common to 1e-4, and common ≥ 0.01 mm³.
  - Cause: OCCT missed a 0.25 ct cushion's prong in the band at its built position (common 0, 2 solids), but found the 0.33 mm³ overlap with the prong moved 0.001 mm either way along any axis. A fuzzy tolerance up to 1e-3 mm did not help.
  - Counted as `shiftedRetries` and recorded by the audit. In the final matrix it fired on **no** design, so it is a guard only.
